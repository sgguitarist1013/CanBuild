Data quality and limitations
    Financial underwriting assumptions

    Construction, architecture, engineering, permitting, financing, carrying costs, contingency and sales commissions must be supported by current, project-specific sources. A cost or ROI is withheld when the building program or valuation is unverified.

    Built for decisions, not guesses.

    CanBuild is an independent preliminary research tool. It is not a survey, appraisal, municipal zoning determination, construction estimate, permit approval or guarantee of buildability. Massachusetts parcel records may be older than the latest municipal records. Zoning amendments, overlays, historic controls and legal unit counts require independent verification. Consult local authorities and qualified professionals before making financial or construction decisions.
