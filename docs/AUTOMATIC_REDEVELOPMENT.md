# Automatic redevelopment screen

The property endpoint populates a preliminary for-sale redevelopment screen for Cambridge C-1 parcels where the existing geometric rule engine can produce an envelope. Other zoning coverage does not imply a usable development envelope. The default report requires no inputs. An optional manual condo tool is available on reports without an automatic estimate.

## Program and revenue

Compare the base four-story envelope and, where the parcel passes the existing lot-area screen, the conditional six-story inclusionary envelope. Each uses the upper geometric area sensitivity. Rank by the highest modeled surplus in the upside case (or remaining proceeds before acquisition when acquisition is unavailable). A conditional case is not an entitlement. The downside case stresses costs and sale prices for the same program; it is not the lower footprint case.

Allow 18% of modeled gross area for common/service space. Fit whole units using assumed interior areas of 700, 1,050, and 1,400 sq ft and a target mix of 20% one-bedroom, 50% two-bedroom, and 30% three-bedroom, as in Table 34 of the [Cambridge September 2026 study](https://www.cambridgema.gov/-/media/Files/CDD/Housing/Inclusionary/cambridgeinclusionaryhousingstudy2026.pdf). Deduct unallocated area separately. Where the modeled project has at least ten units, gross envelope exceeds 10,000 sq ft, or the option is conditional on inclusionary housing, reserve whole units covering at least 20% of the modeled unit area. Gross area is a conservative screening proxy for the ordinance's residential-space trigger. Confirm the actual statutory area calculation, exemptions, bedroom distribution and project approval under the [Cambridge inclusionary program](https://www.cambridgema.gov/CDD/housing/fordevelopersandpropmanagers/inclusionarydevelopers). Restricted-unit revenue is excluded until project-specific price limits are available.

The Cambridge screen uses the [city’s FY2026 assessor database](https://data.cambridgema.gov/d/waa7-ibdu). The search is citywide because unit coordinates are often missing. The assessor database reports a unit's latest sale at the time of the release, so it may omit subsequent sales and earlier transactions. Require a residential condominium, positive living area, sale within three years, recorded book/page, and closing in the reported year built or the following year. Reject possible bundled sales and count at most one sale per shared parcel identifier in each bedroom group as a project proxy. Assessor fields do not independently verify first sale, restricted pricing, arm’s-length status, condition or concessions. For each size, use the middle half of rates when at least three project proxies fall within 20% of the modeled unit area, with matching bedroom counts when reported. Otherwise use the Cambridge study Table 33 submarket averages by bedroom count. An incomplete query does not set the rate.

## Costs

Study Table 27 supplies the $320/sq ft wood-frame and $372/sq ft podium hard-cost benchmarks. The downside case adds 10%. Ordinary demolition/abatement, sitework, civil/offsite and utility work, escalation and contingency are separately budgeted assumptions. Design, engineering, permitting, due diligence, legal, accounting, development management, insurance, security and temporary utilities are allowances derived from the project's modeled hard costs. The screen budgets for taxes, sales-period carry and initial condo reserves by unit. It includes acquisition and buyer closing costs when an assessor value is available, explicitly as a proxy. It includes brokerage, marketing, closing, deeds excise and concessions as a combined sale-cost allowance rather than a statutory calculation.

Financing uses the study's 7.5% construction rate and 30% equity benchmark (Table 30) with assumed fees, construction and sales periods, and draw balances. The [OCC construction lending handbook](https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/files/commercial-real-estate-lending/index-commercial-real-estate-lending.html) informs the interest reserve approach. There is no permanent loan in this for-sale case. No dedicated parking construction or subsidy is assumed. Verify site-dependent costs such as extraordinary remediation, tenant relocation, unusual foundations, offsite work and parking. These can materially change the result. Developer income tax, equity distributions and preferred returns are outside the modeled project surplus.
