# Municipal zoning and sale source review

Reviewed November 9, 2026. The ranks below follow the [FY2025 mean single-family assessed-value queue](MUNICIPAL_ROLLOUT.md). A source or parcel case can support research before its rules are implemented. An assessor transfer, registry index entry, or monthly sales report is a lead until the exact MassLandRecords instrument is checked. Record property identity, closed sale date and consideration, transaction conditions, and fit to the subject separately. The cases below remain open for review.

## 1. Chilmark

- **Zoning.** Use the [town's maps and bylaw index](https://www.chilmarkma.gov/1865/Complete-List-of-Zoning-Bylaws), [Article 6 dimensional rules](https://www.chilmarkma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/11184/Town-of-Chilmark-Zoning-Bylaws-Article-6-08072026), and [Attorney General approval postings](https://www.chilmarkma.gov/2023/MA-Attorney-General-Bylaw-Submissions-De). A spatial intersection of the FY2026 MassGIS parcel polygon for `24_204_0` with the [county base layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/FNsEJ848HT5uDOHD/arcgis/rest/services/duk_zoning/FeatureServer/1) returned one historical feature, object 9, code `62AR IIB`, with source date April 1, 2004. Reproduce with the parcel polygon in EPSG:4326 and the county layer's intersects operation. Reconcile base boundaries and overlays with the [town map sheets](https://www.chilmarkma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/11187/Zoning-Bylaws-MAPS-1-4-2026) and amendments. Current district, buildout and approval path remain unresolved.
- **Parcel.** The [FY2026 MassGIS assessor layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/hGdibHYSPO59RG1h/ArcGIS/rest/services/Massachusetts_Property_Tax_Parcels/FeatureServer/0) returns a single 308 South Road parcel, map-lot `24_204_0` and location ID `M_266364_788837`, using `CITY='CHILMARK' AND MAP_PAR_ID='24_204_0'`.
- **Subject transfer.** The [September 2026 Dukes report](https://massrods.com/dukes/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/10/September2026.pdf) points to book 1746, page 328. A Recorded Land Book Search in [Dukes MassLandRecords](https://www.masslandrecords.com/Dukes/D/Default.aspx) confirmed deed document 4343, recorded September 18, 2026. The address detail labels it “Beach Lot.” The underlying legal description was unavailable in the review, leaving the parcels conveyed, price allocation and transaction conditions unresolved. Keep it out of subject pricing and comparable sales pending exact parcel and consideration review.
- **Sales.** The [Dukes Registry monthly sales reports](https://massrods.com/dukes/sales-reports/) supply recorded book/page or registered instrument leads. Open the corresponding [Dukes MassLandRecords record](https://www.masslandrecords.com/Dukes/D/Default.aspx), then match living area and room counts using assessor evidence.

## 2. Nantucket

- **Parcel.** 5 Bartlett Farm Road is map 65, lot 14. The [FY2026 MassGIS assessor layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/hGdibHYSPO59RG1h/ArcGIS/rest/services/Massachusetts_Property_Tax_Parcels/FeatureServer/0) returns a unique row for `CITY='NANTUCKET' AND MAP_PAR_ID='65 14'`, with assessor use reported as mixed use.
- **Zoning.** The [2026 warrant, Articles 60–61](https://www.nantucket-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/54568/2026-Annual-Town-Meeting-Warrant-with-Finance-Committee-Motions-PDF) and [town GIS amendment maps](https://nantucket-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/54517/2026-Annual-Town-Meeting-All-Maps-PDF) identify the RC-2 to CN change and the Country to Town overlay change. The [town meeting summary](https://www.nantucket-ma.gov/3861/Town-Meeting-Summary) records adoption. Confirm state approval and the effective Clerk-filed map before using these changes in a report.
- **Sales.** FY2026 MassGIS reports a March 30, 2011 transfer lead at book 1274, page 169, with zero consideration in the assessor record. Review the instrument in [Nantucket MassLandRecords](https://www.masslandrecords.com/Nantucket/D/Default.aspx). This lead is insufficient for subject pricing or comparable sales.

## 3. Brookline

- **Zoning.** The [town zoning web map configuration](https://gis.brooklinema.gov/portal/sharing/rest/content/items/672614211a2346f3b8bd136811c8baae/data?f=json) identifies [base zoning](https://gis.brooklinema.gov/server/rest/services/Planning/FeatureServer/9), [overlays](https://gis.brooklinema.gov/server/rest/services/Planning/FeatureServer/10), and [parcel zoning](https://gis.brooklinema.gov/server/rest/services/Planning/FeatureServer/11). Reconcile those layers with the [town amendment bulletin](https://www.brooklinema.gov/2369/New-Zoning-By-Laws-Bulletin), including the September 21, 2026 Attorney General approvals for accessory dwelling units and inclusionary zoning. A parcel-level cross-check is still open.
- **Sales.** The [town's calendar-year 2025 sales map](https://brookline.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/interactivelegend/index.html?appid=8eb160006641461c8774bf962c15123b) separates condominium from single- and multifamily sale leads. Match them to the [Norfolk Registry records](https://www.norfolkdeeds.org/research/begin-online-research), exact parcel or unit, and reported room counts. The municipal map alone does not establish transaction conditions.

## 4. Edgartown

- **Zoning and parcels.** The [town permitting GIS parcel layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/FNsEJ848HT5uDOHD/ArcGIS/rest/services/ViewPoint_Edgartown/FeatureServer/1) can link a map-lot and assessor-use category. Use its zoning attribute as a search lead until the town's official zoning map, current bylaw and overlays corroborate the parcel.
- **Sales.** 69 School St matches parcel `20D-152` in the town layer. The [September 2026 Dukes Registry sales report](https://massrods.com/dukes/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/10/September2026.pdf) lists the address at book 1745, page 24, recorded September 2. Confirm the exact [Dukes MassLandRecords instrument](https://www.masslandrecords.com/Dukes/D/Default.aspx) and the assessor property characteristics before using it in pricing.

## 5. Weston

- **Zoning and parcel.** The [2026 consolidated zoning bylaw](https://www.westonma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/44156/Zoning-By-Law-2026-with-Amendments-PDF), §XIII.B.2, divides 133 Boston Post Road, assessor map 24 lot 1 (MassGIS `024_001_000`), among three Overlay A subdistricts. Obtain the operative map and use the applicable subdistrict boundaries and site-plan criteria before modeling a footprint. Keep the office parcel separate from residential sale leads.
- **Sales.** The [calendar-year 2025 sales list for FY2027 assessments](https://www.westonma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/44377/Calendar-Year-2025-Property-Sales-For-Fiscal-Year-2027-Assessments-PDF) supplies map/block/lot, sale date, reported living area and book/page. Verify the instrument in [Middlesex South MassLandRecords](https://www.masslandrecords.com/MiddlesexSouth/D/Default.aspx) and obtain bed and bath counts separately from the [assessor](https://www.westonma.gov/149/Assessors-Office).

## 7. Wellesley

- **Zoning.** The app already connects the town [GIS base and overlay layers](https://services6.arcgis.com/f6G5SbcwuEVmR1CW/arcgis/rest/services/Open_Data_Layers/FeatureServer). Check the [consolidated bylaw](https://www.wellesleyma.gov/281/Zoning-Bylaws) with the separately posted [Attorney General-approved 2026 Residential Incentive Overlay amendment](https://wellesleyma.gov/m/newsflash/home/detail/2973) before treating a mapped overlay as an approval path.
- **Parcel and sales.** The [FY2026 assessed-values list](https://www.wellesleyma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/22395/residential-one-liner) identifies 23–27 Washington Street as map 34, parcel 17. Use the [Norfolk Registry research entry point](https://www.norfolkdeeds.org/research/begin-online-research) to check deed evidence and confirm that a transfer relates to the exact assessed parcel or unit.
